About me

I see beauty in things others
walk past

I’m Moraa, founder of Mariposa Co., based in Boston, Massachusetts. By day I work in tech, and somewhere along the way I realized that what I love most isn’t writing code. It's making things feel good and look good, whether that's a space, a website, or a journey.

Mariposa means butterfly. It’s transformation, movement, beauty in unexpected places. That’s the energy I bring to every project and every itinerary I put together. I want people to love where they live, how they show up online, and where they go next.
